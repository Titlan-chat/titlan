# Contributing to Titlan

Thanks for your interest in Titlan!

**External code contributions are not yet accepted.** The project's
contribution and licensing policy (including a possible CLA) is still being
finalized; until that is settled, unsolicited pull requests may be closed
without review.

In the meantime:

- **Issues and discussion are welcome** — bug reports, questions, and design
  feedback all help.
- **Security reports** go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never through
  public issues.
